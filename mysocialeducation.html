my theory for social education

The core catalyst in the construction of a coherent society is the establishment of an exemplary edict of education. In our current American society, education is simply a means to furthermore increase capital and monetary standing in society. I firmly believe this is completely upside down from what our goals should be concerning the education of our youth. I propse a simple principle on which a system of education can be constructed that aims to serve as not a provider of social mobility, but social belonging and, more importantly, self-purpose. This principle is that every student going through an educational system should have three vocations: an academic, an athletic, and an artistic. The balance of these three deeply personal items will lead to the development of extremely self-inteligent and aware students who understand nuanced topics across multiple different realms. These students will be not only externally aware of the world around them, but intrisically aware of themselves and how they interact with the world around them. The academic vocation gives them an enjoyable and interesting pathway to contribute to society. As they say, "enjoy what you do and you never work a day in your life." Through this establishment, members of a society will feel more welcomed and develop a sense of belonging with their communities. The atheltic vocation allows students to discover a way to find their physical limits and gain higher control of their physical body through different atheltical programs that push their limits and challenge them to overcome obstacles. The artistic vocation allows students to develop a premier sense of natural empathy and understanding of the thoughts and feelings surrounding humanity and how they can be manifested in artistic forms that display true human ingenuity. Through the development of programs surrounding these three vocations, not ony will society benefit with more impactful and thoughtful members, but the members themselves will lead meaningful and exciting lives that further better society. This, however, should be implemented with other forms of government that promote social cohesion, specifically through infrastructure and urban planning. Human interactions need to be maximized so that every individual can develop their own niche of belonging in the society and adequetly contribute to it as well as have to society adequetly contribute to themselves.
- liluzibear's theory of social education
