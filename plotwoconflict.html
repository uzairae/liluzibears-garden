this was such a wonderful article. it began by introducing the fact that western literature often is built on the principle of conflict: one party prevailing over another. this literary concept spreads throughout western literature not only in in fiction, but in nonfiction and even as far as philosophy. not only does it dominate literature, but it begins to dictate western ways of life as well. the need to constantly be better than others. the need to put another party down to prove yourself worth, the need to keep growing no matter what. then the author introduces an alternative to this principle through an example of eastern writing where conflict is not centered. this was very, very interesting.

however, the author then takes a turn. the whole article begins to be referred to in a third-person perspective: a meta-analysis of sorts. the author concedes that his analysis comparing these two is in of itself a product of the western assumptions revolving literature.
