the day

I dream of a day where the very lights illuminating my face as I write this go dark. The night sky, for the first time in decades, prevails as the primary beacon on the Earth. The wawter pipes begin to run brown ith the unprocessed recirculations polluting the source. Flame is only produced by the oxygen in our atmosphere. The complex governments and societies we developed become useless as humanity delves into a new age of anarchism. The collapse of law leads to the existing social cohesion to fail and only social adhesion that we have relentlessly fostered through our immoral politics reign as the motivating factor for all decisions. The very precautins we developed to protect ourselves eventually develop our collective demise, as we alienate one another and avoid contact. The spirit of huminaty, of coexistance, of empathy, of trust is lost to time. The rebuilding of these cohesive traits in cohesive societies is inhibited and will be long overdue once it returns due to the residue of breeding skepticism and paranoia, overshadoing generations of natural empathy for members of the humankind. REMOVE HATE - ACCEPT LOVE.
